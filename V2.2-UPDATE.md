# Badminton Journal V2.2 — Training Sessions

## What changed
- Coach now creates the official training dates in **Training**.
- Students cannot choose arbitrary dates anymore.
- Attendance and reflections are linked to a coach-created training session.
- An attended player can check in first and complete the reflection later.
- Reflection window is 7 calendar days including the training day. Example: Monday training closes Sunday 11:59 PM Singapore time.
- Students can report an absence for an upcoming session or during the open submission window.
- Coach Attendance page uses a session dropdown rather than a free-form date picker.

## Required Firebase update
Replace your current Firestore Rules with the new `firestore.rules` included in this package and click **Publish**.

A new collection called `trainingSessions` will be created automatically when the coach adds the first training date.

## First use
1. Publish the new Firestore rules.
2. Upload the V2.2 website files to GitHub.
3. Log in as Coach.
4. Open **Training**.
5. Click **Add training date** and add the term's sessions.
6. Students will then see only those official sessions.

Existing old reflections/attendance remain readable. New submissions use the new session-based system.
